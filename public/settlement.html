The basics

What is a payment gateway settlement?

A settlement is when the money your customers paid lands in your bank account.

In between, the payment is confirmed, the funds are processed and the amount is sent to you. Once you know where a payment is on that journey, planning your cash flow gets a lot easier.

Ask our team →

What is a payment gateway settlement?

A payment gateway settlement is when the money your customers paid lands in your bank account. In between, the payment is confirmed, the funds are processed and the amount is sent to you as a settlement credit.

How do I track a settlement from payment to bank credit?

Pay101 records every transaction with its status, groups confirmed payments into settlement activity you can follow, and lets you trace each bank credit back to the payments behind it. Transactions, payouts and settlements sit together in one view.

How long does a payment gateway settlement take?

Timing can vary, which is why Pay101 shows you where your funds are at every step instead of leaving you to guess. For the settlement timeline that applies to your business, talk to our team.

How do settlements make reconciliation easier?

Clear settlement records make it simpler to match your bank statement. Because each credit links to the payments it covers, your finance team can use payment reconciliation without hunting through spreadsheets.

What happens if a settlement is pending or delayed?

Pay101 helps you spot pending or delayed settlements early, before they hurt your cash flow. Delays and gaps show up while they’re still easy to fix, so you can plan payouts and expenses with confidence.

Who on my team can see settlement details?

Role-aware access keeps settlement details with the people who need them, whether that’s finance leads, operations teams or leadership. Your funds also move through protected, security-focused workflows at every stage. See our security approach for more.
