FAQ

Solutions questions

What solutions does Pay101 offer?

Pay101 is a B2B payments platform that offers three connected solutions: Payin to collect money from customers, Payout to send money to bank accounts, and Settlement to track funds and reconcile transactions, all on one platform.

What is the difference between payin, payout and settlement?

Payin is money coming in from customers. Payout is money going out to partners, vendors or customers. Settlement is the process of moving collected funds to your account and matching them to your transactions.

Can I use Payin and Payout together?

Yes. Many businesses collect online payments from customers and pay out to vendors, creators or partners using the same Pay101 platform.

Does Pay101 have a bulk payout service?

Pay101 supports payouts to bank accounts by IMPS, NEFT or RTGS, so you can pay vendors, partners and customers without handling each transfer manually. IMPS transfers are real-time and available 24x7, and payout automation through the API helps you avoid manual work. Confirm bulk payout options for your setup with our team.

How can I track my settlements?

Pay101 helps you see what has been paid, what is pending and when funds are available, so your team can reconcile settlements against transactions.

Who are Pay101 solutions built for?

Growing businesses, with a focus on media, gaming and digital businesses that need to collect and send money reliably.
