FAQ

Payin questions

What is Pay101 Payin?

Pay101 Payin is a payment gateway for businesses to collect online payments from customers. Customers can pay by UPI, cards, wallets or IMPS, and you can accept payments through your website or app, or share a payment link.

How do I accept online payments with Pay101?

You create an order, the customer pays with their preferred method, Pay101 sends the result to your server, and you verify the status before crediting the customer. Approved partners connect through the API, and our team supports you from setup to launch.

Does Pay101 offer a payment gateway UPI option?

Yes. Customers can pay by UPI through Pay101, alongside cards, wallets and IMPS bank transfers, so you can offer a payment gateway UPI checkout and other methods through one integration.

Can I accept credit and debit cards with Pay101?

Yes. Pay101 works as a credit card payment gateway and also accepts debit cards, with a secure checkout for your customers. Card availability depends on your business and market, so confirm it with our team.

What are Pay101 payment links, and do they need integration?

A payment link is a link you share with a customer so they can pay you online. It needs no integration. You send the link, the customer pays with an available method, and you see the payment status.

How do I know an online payment was successful?

Pay101 sends a callback to your server when a payment completes. Before you credit the customer, confirm the final status with Pay101. Never rely on the callback alone.
