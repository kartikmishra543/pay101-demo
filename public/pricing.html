What Is on Every Quote

So you can compare us with any other provider, each of these is stated clearly.

  • Per-transaction rate for each payment method
  • Setup fee, if there is one
  • Monthly or platform fee, if there is one
  • Payout and transfer charges
  • Currency conversion, where it applies
  • Taxes that apply to your business

What Affects your Rate

Rates are not one-size-fits-all. These are the main factors.

  • Your industry, since some carry more risk than others
  • The markets you sell in and the payment methods you need
  • Your monthly volume and average payment size
  • How quickly you need settlement
  • Your payout needs and integration setup

FAQ

Questions About Pricing

Why is there no price on the page?

Rates depend on your industry, markets, payment methods and volume. A quote gives you the real number for your business, instead of a headline rate that may not apply to you.

What does a payment gateway cost?

It depends on your payment methods, monthly volume, settlement speed and setup. That is why we quote each business individually and itemise every fee.

Do you offer fixed plans?

We do not use a fixed price list. Your quote is built around your industry, markets, payment methods and volume, and it lists every fee.

Are there setup or monthly fees?

That depends on your setup. Any setup or monthly fee is listed in your quote before you decide.

Do you support my industry?

We work with media and entertainment, gaming and digital businesses. Tell us about your business in the form below and we will confirm what we can offer.

Which payment methods and countries are available?

Availability depends on your market and setup. We confirm the options for your business in your quote.

How quickly can I get started?

After you accept your quote, our team guides you through onboarding. Timing depends on verification and the integration you choose.

What happens when my volume grows?

Talk to us and we will review your rates and setup as your volume grows.

Can I compare your quote with other providers?

Yes. Every quote lists each type of fee, so you can compare like for like.
