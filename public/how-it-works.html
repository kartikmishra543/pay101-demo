Need help?

Frequently asked questions

What is Collect?

Collect is the area for accepting customer payments and reviewing their transaction status. Available payment methods depend on your platform configuration.

What is Pay Out?

Pay Out helps businesses send funds to eligible beneficiaries and monitor the progress of those transfers.

What is Settle?

Settle helps users review settlement records and understand the status and timing of incoming funds. Actual timelines depend on your payment arrangements.

Where can I get help while using the dashboard?

Use the getting-started guide, contextual help near a field, or your platform’s support channel.
