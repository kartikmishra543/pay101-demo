Who we are

Your partner in digital payment services

Pay101 exists because payments should not slow a business down. As companies grow, collecting money, paying partners and checking settlements often end up in different tools. We bring those pieces together.

We provide digital payment services that help merchants organize transactions, connect supported payment services and see relevant payment information in one place, with a focus on clarity, security and local payment methods.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Pay101.

What is Pay101?

Pay101 is a fintech payment company that brings payins, payouts and settlements into one connected experience. It is built around the way businesses and customers pay in different markets.

What does Pay101 do?

Pay101 provides digital payment services that help businesses collect payments, pay partners, vendors and customers, and track settlements. Merchants can organize transactions and see relevant payment information in one place.

Who is Pay101 built for?

Pay101 is built for growing businesses, with a focus on media, gaming and digital businesses that need to collect and send money across markets.

Which payment methods does Pay101 support?

Pay101 supports UPI, cards, wallets and IMPS transfers. Availability depends on the market and on your business, so please confirm the methods you need with our team.

Which markets does Pay101 serve?

Pay101 is built around local payment options in India, Nepal, Bhutan, Brazil and Australia, and is exploring more markets. Availability must be confirmed with Pay101 for your business.

What are Pay101's vision and mission?

Our vision is to be a trusted digital payment infrastructure that gives businesses one clear, connected view of money moving in, out and across markets. Our mission is to simplify collections, payouts and settlements with familiar local payment options, clear workflows and developer-friendly technology.

How does Pay101 approach security?

Security is one of our core values. We aim to handle payment information carefully in everything we build. To learn more about our approach, visit our Security page or contact our team.

How do I get started with Pay101?

Contact our team and tell us about your business and the markets you want to reach. We will help you choose the right payment methods and plan your setup.
