1. Introduction This Cookie Policy explains how Pay101 (“Pay101”, “we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on its public website and, where applicable, its merchant dashboard.

A cookie is a small file stored on a device by a website. Similar technologies may include pixels, local storage, or software development tools that store or access information on a device.

This policy must be aligned with the cookies and technologies actually installed on Pay101’s website and dashboard. Listing a technology here does not mean Pay101 currently uses it.

2. Categories of Cookies Strictly necessary cookies: These may support essential website functions, secure sessions, login, authentication, load balancing, and security. Where legally exempt, they may be used without consent.

Preference or functional cookies: These may remember settings or user preferences. Where consent is required, they should only be activated after consent.

Analytics or performance cookies: If used, these help understand visits, page performance, and usage patterns. They should not be activated before the consent required by applicable law.

Advertising or marketing cookies: If used, these may measure campaigns or support advertising. They should be activated only after the required consent.

Third-party technologies: Embedded content, analytics, support tools, or advertising providers may set or access their own technologies, subject to their configuration and applicable law.

3. How Cookies May Be Used

Depending on the technologies implemented, cookies may help Pay101 maintain website and dashboard functionality, secure sessions, remember preferences, understand site performance, investigate technical problems, or measure marketing activity.

Pay101 should document the purpose, provider, cookie name, and expiry of each non-essential cookie in its consent settings or cookie inventory.

4. Consent and Managing Preferences Where applicable law requires consent, Pay101 will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

A suitable consent tool should allow visitors to accept, reject, or customise non-essential categories and to change or withdraw their choices later. Non-essential technologies should not run before the required consent is obtained.

Visitors can also use browser settings to delete or block cookies. Blocking essential cookies may affect sign-in, security, or other website functions. Browser controls do not necessarily replace a website consent tool where one is legally required.

5. Cookie Retention and Third Parties Session cookies generally expire when the browser session ends. Persistent cookies remain until their defined expiry or deletion. Actual durations vary by cookie and must be confirmed through a website and dashboard cookie audit.

Where third-party services are used, their providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Pay101 should identify relevant providers and explain any applicable data transfers in its Privacy Policy and consent interface.

6. Personal Information Information obtained through cookies or similar technologies may include IP address, device and browser information, pages viewed, interactions, approximate location, and identifiers.

Where this information is personal data, Pay101 will handle it in accordance with applicable data protection law and its Privacy Policy.

7. Changes and Contact Pay101 may update this policy when its website, dashboard, technologies, or legal requirements change.
