Vendor and Partner Payments

Send money to partners, vendors and customers

Most businesses pay all kinds of people: suppliers, service partners, creators, customers waiting on a refund. When each payment means another bank login and another manual entry, mistakes slip in and payments run late.

With Pay101, you create a payout, pick the beneficiary and send it from the same platform where you collect payments. If you pay on a regular schedule, automated payouts take the repetitive work off your team. Payout automation becomes part of your routine, not one more thing to remember.

Bank Transfers

IMPS, NEFT and RTGS workflows

Not every payment needs the same route. Pay101 supports the transfer modes Indian businesses use every day:

  • IMPS for quick transfers when the person shouldn’t have to wait. An IMPS money transfer works well for urgent vendor payments, creator payouts and refunds.
  • NEFT for regular, planned payments like vendor invoices and partner settlements.
  • RTGS for large-value transfers that you want to move in a single, direct payment.

You pick the right mode for each payout, and the workflow stays the same whichever one you choose. That makes Pay101 a dependable instant payout payment gateway for payments that can’t wait, and a steady option for the ones that can.

FAQ

Payout questions

What is Pay101 payout?

Pay101 payout lets businesses send money to vendors, partners, creators and customers from the same platform where they collect payments. You can pay by IMPS, NEFT or RTGS and track the status of every payout.

Which transfer mode should I use: IMPS, NEFT or RTGS?

Use IMPS for quick transfers, such as urgent vendor payments, creator payouts and refunds. Use NEFT for regular, planned payments like vendor invoices and partner settlements. Use RTGS for large-value transfers that you want to move in a single, direct payment. An IMPS money transfer works well when the person shouldn’t have to wait.

Can I pay many people at once with Pay101?

Yes. The bulk payout service lets you pay many beneficiaries in a single step. Prepare your list, send it and follow every payment’s status from the same screen. Ask our team how bulk lists are uploaded for your account.

Can I automate payouts?

Yes. Automated payouts take the repetitive work off your team if you pay on a regular schedule. As a payout API provider, Pay101 also lets approved partners send payouts from their own systems through the API.

How do I know a payout was successful?

Pay101 shows the status of each payout without leaving the platform, as SUCCESS, PENDING or FAILURE. Check the status with your own order ID, and mark a payout as paid only once it shows SUCCESS.

Is Pay101 an instant payout payment gateway?

Pay101 works as an instant payout payment gateway for payments that can’t wait. Payouts by IMPS are real-time and available 24x7, while NEFT and RTGS suit planned and large-value payments. Availability depends on your setup, so confirm details with our team.
