Which industries does Pay101 payment gateway support?

Pay101 is a payment gateway for businesses in digital services, gaming and media & entertainment. It also works with many other digital businesses, so if you don’t see your industry, talk to our team and we’ll show you the fit.

How does Pay101 work as a payment gateway for digital services?

Pay101 is a merchant payment gateway and payout platform in one. You collect client payments through reliable online payment processors, use automated payouts for freelancers, vendors and partners, and track every transaction, payout and settlement in one view.

Can gaming companies use Pay101 for in-game purchases and top-ups?

Yes. Pay101 offers an instant payment gateway experience with protected payment flows for the spikes that gaming brings. It supports gaming payment processing for in-game purchases and top-ups, plus payout automation and bulk payouts for developers and creators.

How can media and entertainment businesses collect subscriptions and pay creators?

As a digital media payment gateway, Pay101 lets you collect subscription payments for content and events, then pay creators and partners in one go with the bulk payout service. Both sides of the money flow stay in one place.

Does Pay101 support bulk payouts and bank transfers?

Yes. Pay101 supports payouts to partners, vendors and customers, bank transfers over IMPS, NEFT and RTGS, and bulk payouts for paying many people at once. Role-aware access keeps sensitive payout actions with the right people.

Can I track settlements and reconcile payments in one place?

Yes. Pay101 brings collections, payouts and settlements onto one secure platform, so you can follow settlements from transaction to bank credit and use reconciliation to match settlements to transactions as your volumes grow.
